Eggxiety Privacy Policy

Effective 28 October 2026

About this policy

Eggxiety is a self-care app that helps you manage anxiety with a companion, breathing exercises, journaling and small games. This policy explains what information the app collects, why, who it is shared with, and the choices you have.

Eggxiety is operated by Isaac Joshua H. Imperial, a sole trader based in Australia ("we", "us"). Questions about privacy: isaac.xperial@gmail.com.

This policy applies to the Eggxiety apps for iOS and Android. It takes effect on 28 October 2026.

How we use it

We use your information to run the app for you, and for nothing unrelated to that.

  • Provide the app: show your companion, streaks, journal and progress, and keep them in sync if you reinstall or switch devices.
  • Weekly letters: only if you turn them on. Once a week, the emotions you logged and any notes you wrote that week are sent to our AI provider (Anthropic) to write a short, private letter back to you. Your name, email and date of birth are not included. Letters are off until you turn them on, and you can turn them off at any time in Settings.
  • Subscriptions: confirm whether you have premium and restore purchases.
  • Reminders: schedule the notifications you turn on. These are created on your device.
  • Improve the app: understand which features are used and fix crashes, using usage analytics.
  • Support and safety: read feedback you send, and keep the service secure.

We do not sell your information, use it for advertising, or use your mood or journal data for marketing. We do not use it to train AI models, and our AI provider does not train on it under its commercial terms.

Legal bases (EU and UK): performance of our contract with you (running the app and sync), your consent (health-related journal data, weekly letters and, where required, analytics), and our legitimate interests (security and improving the app).

Storage, security and retention

Your data is encrypted in transit and at rest on our servers. No other user of the app can see your records.

  • In transit: all traffic between the app and our providers uses HTTPS (TLS).
  • On our servers: stored with Supabase in Tokyo, Japan, encrypted at rest. Database access rules mean each account can read and change only its own data.
  • On your device: stored in the app's private storage, protected by your phone's built-in encryption and lock screen.
  • Journal notes: the app also encrypts the notes you write, with a key kept in your phone's secure keystore. Once synced, they are encrypted at rest on our servers but are not end-to-end encrypted, so our systems can read them, for example to write your weekly letter.
  • International transfers: our providers may process data outside your country, including in Japan, where our database is hosted, and in the United States, where our analytics provider stores its data. Where required, transfers rely on safeguards such as the EU Standard Contractual Clauses.

How long we keep it: for as long as your account exists. A guest account with no activity for 90 days, and no active subscription, is deleted automatically along with its data and analytics profile. When you delete your account, your profile, journal entries, weekly letters, progress, subscription record and feedback are deleted from our servers straight away, and the app clears its local copy. We also ask our analytics provider to delete your analytics profile and events, which it completes within about a week. Otherwise, analytics data is kept for up to 1 year and is then deleted. We do not keep backups of our database, so data deleted from our servers cannot be restored.

Your choices and rights

You can delete your account and all its data at any time, in the app or by email (see Delete your account below).

  • Delete your account: Settings > Account > Delete account. This works for guest accounts too.
  • Notifications: turn reminders on or off in the app or in your phone's settings.
  • Analytics: turn off usage analytics in Settings at any time. While it is off, the app sends no usage events.
  • Subscriptions: cancel through the App Store or Google Play. Deleting your account does not cancel a subscription.
  • Access, correction and export: edit your profile in the app, or email isaac.xperial@gmail.com for a copy of your data or any other request. We reply within 30 days.

Depending on where you live (for example the EU, UK or California), you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict or port your data, to object to processing, and to withdraw consent at any time. You can also complain to your local data protection authority. We do not sell or share personal information for cross-context behavioural advertising.

Delete your account

You can delete your Eggxiety account in the app, or by email if you no longer have the app.

  1. In the app: Settings > Account > Delete account. Your data is deleted from our servers straight away and the app clears its local copy. This works for guest accounts too.
  2. Without the app: email isaac.xperial@gmail.com from the email address you signed in with, with the subject "Delete my account". We confirm the request and delete the account within 30 days.

Either way, we delete your account, profile, journal entries, weekly letters, progress, subscription record and feedback, and ask our analytics provider to delete your analytics profile and events. We keep nothing afterwards, as we do not keep database backups.

A guest account has no email address, so we cannot match an email request to it. Delete a guest account in the app before you uninstall. Otherwise it is deleted automatically after 90 days without activity. Deleting your account does not cancel a subscription; cancel it through the App Store or Google Play.

Children

Eggxiety is not meant for children under 13, and the app does not accept a date of birth under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has given us information, email isaac.xperial@gmail.com and we will delete it. In the EU and UK, users under 16 should use the app with a parent or guardian's consent.

Not a medical service

Eggxiety supports self-care and is not a medical device, diagnosis or treatment. If you are in crisis, contact your local emergency number or find a helpline at findahelpline.com.

Changes to this policy

We will post any changes here and update the effective date. If a change is significant, we will tell you in the app before it takes effect.
